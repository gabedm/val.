## Billing limits

Annual monthly equivalents have no invented upfront bill. Variable baskets have no invented monthly total. Explicit introductory VPN terms are retained as term charges; ExpressVPN's documented later annual renewal is separate. SimplyCook's £11.98 already includes £1.99 postage. Asana's £23.18 minimum monthly base is arithmetic for two seats, not a full checkout total.

## Selected exclusions

Napster's current site describes an AI service, so it was not retained as a music-streaming candidate. Reflect's current positioning no longer supplied a clean paid subscription candidate. Soundtrap's readable price-bearing promotion was from 2019. Artlist, VEED, Epidemic Sound, Gamma, Tresorit and several meal providers did not yield sufficiently clear selected-plan prices in this pass; they were replaced with sourced candidates. The Atlantic and Telegraph store listings did not establish a clean selected billing interval, so other publications were used. These exclusions do not imply the services are unavailable or free.

## Evidence limits

Official stores often retain several legacy offers. A named monthly/yearly item establishes a published price point, not eligibility for that offer. Historical issuer/provider announcements establish a real historical rate, not a current price. Provider descriptions establish claims, not measured quality or medical, privacy, learning or investment outcomes. No account access, API credentials, purchases, cancellations or migrations were exercised for the 200 services.
