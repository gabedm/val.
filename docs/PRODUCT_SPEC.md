# Product

Val compares subscriptions around the person's confirmed needs.

1. Connect PayPal and import supported payment records.
2. Identify the products and ask what the person uses them for.
3. Confirm the saved needs before research starts.
4. Compare provider-sourced offers, including billing terms and compromises.
5. Save a checking schedule per subscription.
6. Prepare a supported switch, request approval, verify activation, then cancel and verify the old renewal.

The tabs are Val, Subscriptions and Account. They read the same saved portfolio. A newly imported subscription returns to the chat for its own needs.

Research failures stay separate from a completed search with no suitable offer. A catalogue entry is reference data, not a financial integration. The app never promises universal coverage or guaranteed savings.
