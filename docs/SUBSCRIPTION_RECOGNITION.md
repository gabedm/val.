# Recognition

Val first checks normalised names and explicit catalogue aliases. Ambiguous labels stay ambiguous.

Unfamiliar products use bounded provider research through the configured model. This lookup receives the product label and market, not payment identifiers or raw statements. Retrieved HTTPS evidence must support the identity.

Public product facts can be cached. Customer clarifications stay private. Missing evidence leads to a short question rather than a guessed product.

Product facts do not establish the person's plan, bill or needs. Saved needs require confirmation. The same process handles catalogue and unfamiliar subscriptions.

Set `VAL_LIVE_DISCOVERY=false` to disable live research. Tests simulate unknown products, spelling variations, ambiguity, failures and owner isolation.
