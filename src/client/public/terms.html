Using Val

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Your decisions

Val helps compare subscription fit and value. Review the actual costs, eligibility, renewal terms and recommendation before choosing a change. An estimate is not a guaranteed saving. You remain in control of approvals.

PayPal

Signing in identifies your Val account. It does not approve a purchase, recurring charge, cancellation or access to your wallet. Payment approval happens separately on PayPal.

Coverage

Automated switching is limited to supported connections. Listing a service does not mean Val can buy or cancel it. Information and availability may change.

Access

Use only accounts you are authorised to access. Keep review credentials and recovery codes private. Val is a prototype and may be unavailable. Review a supported switch before approving it.
