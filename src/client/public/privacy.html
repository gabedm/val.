Privacy

Updated 12 November 2026. This notice covers this installation of Val.

What Val stores

Subscription records, billing evidence, chat answers, confirmed needs, comparisons and checking schedules. Access is tied to your account. Session cookies keep you signed in and protect requests.

PayPal

PayPal handles your password. Login provides identity, not wallet history or payment approval. Val keeps a keyed identity reference and its own session; the sign-in access token is not retained.

AI research

The configured AI service receives relevant product names, costs and usage answers to interpret needs and compare offers. Gemini uses Google Cloud; an operator may select Anthropic or OpenAI instead. Raw statements and account identifiers are excluded from recommendation research.

Optional browser connections

KERNEL hosts the PayPal browser used for wallet imports. Val reads statements and recurring-payment details, removes temporary reports and retains extracted evidence. No email access is used. Browser capabilities are encrypted in server storage; the authenticated browser has broader access than a read-only API.

Switching uses separate provider browser sessions and redacted page text. Enter credentials and payment approvals on the provider's page. Val requires approval before a supported purchase or cancellation.

Disconnecting

Logout ends the Val session. Disconnect PayPal to remove its retained remote profile and stop that connection's imports. Saved records and uncertain financial outcomes may remain for reconciliation. Contact the installation's operator about retention or deletion.

Use only accounts you are authorised to connect. Keep remote-browser links and recovery codes private.
